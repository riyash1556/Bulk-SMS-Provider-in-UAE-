## Promotional, Transactional, and OTP SMS

A business messaging platform can support different communication purposes.

### Promotional SMS
Promotional messages help businesses share discounts, special offers, new products, and marketing announcements with customers who have agreed to receive them.

### Transactional SMS
Transactional messages provide important information related to customer activities, such as payment confirmations, order updates, and account notifications.

### OTP SMS
One-time passwords are used to verify users during registration, login, and other authentication processes.

Selecting the appropriate message type helps businesses organize their communication more effectively.
